# What Minecraft has that LethalCraft doesn't (yet)

This lists what Minecraft Java Edition (1.21.x) has that LethalCraft does not, checked against the mod's source
(version 1.47.4) on 2026-10-15, and kept up to date as things come in, so the owner can read through and pick what to
add next. It is grouped by category; each line says what the thing is in Minecraft and, where useful, how it might fit
Lethal Company. Bows and arrows, gravel and flint, and chickens and feathers came in with 1.40.0 and are left out.
"(partly: ...)" marks things the mod already has some of.

## Came in since 1.43.0

Things the list still had at 1.43.0 that are in the mod now (a few came in 1.41.0 and 1.42.0 but were still listed).
The version is the one each came in.

- Stray and bogged, with the tipped arrows they drop; baby zombies, chicken jockeys, spider jockeys (1.41.0)
- Charged creepers, and the mob heads they knock off, worn as helmets; endermites; zombified piglins (1.41.0)
- Golden pickaxe, shovel, axe and hoe; the potion of Regeneration from ghast tears (1.41.0)
- Ghasts complete: ghast tears (1.41.0), hitting their fireballs back (1.45.0)
- The Nether bricks family: red, cracked and chiseled, stairs, slabs, walls, fence (1.43.0)
- Java's quasi-connectivity for pistons (1.43.0), and pistons acting on block updates (1.45.0)
- Copper: ore, raw copper, ingots, the weathering and waxed blocks, doors, trapdoors and bulbs; the lightning rod
  (1.44.0)
- Hoppers, droppers, dispensers, the crafter and the composter (1.44.0)
- Rails (plain, powered, detector, activator) and minecarts (plain, chest, hopper, furnace, TNT) (1.44.0)
- Fence gates (1.44.0)
- The lectern, the chiseled bookshelf, the book and quill and the written book (1.44.0)
- The wandering trader, with its trader llamas (1.44.0)

## Mobs

### Hostile, overworld

- **Zombie variants: husk, drowned, zombie villager** (partly, 1.41.0: husks on desert moons, whose hit makes you
  hungry, and drowned on swampy ones are in; missing the zombie villager and curing it, and the drowned's tridents and
  copper ingot drop): a villager turned zombie, cured with a splash potion of Weakness and a golden apple.
- **Cave spider**: small poisonous spider from mineshaft spawners. Fits the facility; needs Poison on the player (witches already give it).
- **Silverfish**: small bug hiding in "infested" stone; more pour out when one is hit. Fits dug stone and the facility.
- **Phantom** (partly, 1.42.0: outdoors after 6 PM rather than after sleepless nights; missing burning in daylight).
- **Creaking**: tree monster that only moves when not looked at, tied to a creaking heart block. Fits moons with trees; close to the Coil-head idea.
- **Guardian / elder guardian**: ocean monument fish that fire a laser; elder gives Mining Fatigue. Needs a water interior.
- **Shulker**: End city box mob whose bullets cause Levitation; drops shulker shells. Needs the End or an End-like interior.
- **Vex**: small flying spirits summoned by an evoker; pass through walls.
- **Pillager**: crossbow raider; patrols and outposts. Needs crossbows.
- **Vindicator**: axe-wielding mansion/raid illager.
- **Evoker**: illager that summons fangs and vexes, drops the totem of undying (the totem is store-only here).
- **Illusioner**: unused illager that makes copies of itself (not in survival in Minecraft either).
- **Ravager**: large raid beast that breaks leaves and crops.
- **Witch** (partly: has the witch, and its spider eye drop since 1.41.0; missing Minecraft's other self-potions such
  as fire resistance and swiftness, and witch huts).
- **Warden**: blind deep-dark mob that tracks by vibration and sound, very strong. A natural fit for Lethal Company's sound-hunting monsters and the facility.
- **Breeze**: trial chamber mob that jumps around and shoots wind charges; drops breeze rods.
- **Slime** (partly: slimes on the moons at night, and since 1.41.0 five times as many on swampy March, standing in for
  swamps; missing slime chunks and Minecraft's swamp rules by moon phase and light).
- **Zombie / skeleton horse**: undead horses; skeleton traps come from lightning.
- **Killer bunny**: hostile rabbit variant (rabbits came in 1.42.0).

### Nether

- **Hoglin** (partly, 1.41.0: in crimson forests, charges and tosses you up, drops porkchops and leather; missing its
  fear of warped fungus (no fungi yet), babies and breeding): big hostile Nether pig.
- **Zoglin**: zombified hoglin that attacks everything.
- **Strider**: lava-walking passive mob; ridden with a saddle and warped fungus on a stick. Needs lava lakes in the Nether.
- **Piglin** (partly: has piglins, brutes, gold armor rule and bartering; missing piglins zombifying outside the Nether, crossbow piglins, and the full barter table since many items don't exist).
- **Wither skeleton** (partly: in the facility and the Nether; drops coal, its skull and, since 1.41.0, bones; missing
  the Wither effect on hit).
- **Happy ghast / dried ghast block**: 1.21.6 rideable ghast grown from a dried ghast block in water, with a harness. A flying mount for the crew.

### End

- **Ender dragon**: the final boss, on the End's central island, healed by end crystals; drops the dragon egg and lots of XP. Needs the End.
- **Shulker** (see Hostile, overworld).
- **Endermen in the End** (endermen exist; the End does not).

### Neutral

- **Iron golem** (partly, 1.42.0: built and fighting; missing its poppy drop (no flowers yet), offering poppies, village spawning).
- **Snow golem** (partly, 1.42.0: built and throwing snowballs; missing its snow trail and shearing off its pumpkin).
- **Polar bear**: snowy-biome bear, hostile when cubs are near. Fits snowy moons.
- **Panda**: bamboo-jungle animal with personalities.
- **Llama / trader llama** (partly, 1.44.0: the wandering trader's two llamas spit, can be freed, tamed and given a
  chest; missing wild llamas and carpets on them): pack animal that spits; carries chests.
- **Goat**: mountain animal that rams; drops goat horns.
- **Bee** (partly: the game's Circuit Bees become one big bee, and since 1.44.0 shears on their hive give honeycomb;
  missing normal bees, hives/nests, pollination and honey bottles).
- **Dolphin**: ocean mob that leads you to treasure and gives Dolphin's Grace.
- **Wolf** (partly: has wolves and taming; missing wolf variants from 1.20.5, wolf armor, collar dyeing, breeding).
- **Fox**: night animal that steals items and sleeps by day; fits outside on wooded moons.
- **Spider by day** is neutral in Minecraft (the mod's spiders are facility monsters).

### Passive / animals

- **Rabbit** (partly, 1.42.0: missing the killer bunny and eating carrot crops).
- **Mooshroom**: mushroom cow; milked for stew. Needs mushrooms.
- **Donkey / mule**: horse relatives that carry a chest.
- **Horse** (partly: coats, markings, armor, breeding, leads came in 1.42.0; missing jump strength and varied speed/health per horse).
- **Camel**: two-rider desert mount that dashes. Fits desert moons.
- **Cat** (partly: has cats in Minecraft's eleven coats, and taming; missing cats bringing gifts at dawn, breeding,
  and the ocelot).
- **Ocelot**: untameable jungle cat that scares creepers.
- **Parrot** (partly: five colors and dancing came in 1.42.0; it already imitates a monster that's near with that
  mob's own sound, pitched up (1.37.0), so this may be complete) (check).
- **Turtle**: beach animal; eggs, scute, turtle helmet.
- **Frog / tadpole**: swamp animals in three temperature variants; eat small slimes and magma cubes to drop froglights.
- **Axolotl**: water animal that fights drowned and guardians; caught in a bucket.
- **Glow squid**: glowing ocean squid dropping glow ink sacs (squid came in 1.42.0).
- **Fish mobs: cod, salmon, tropical fish, pufferfish** (partly: caught by fishing as items; missing the swimming mobs and fish buckets).
- **Bat**: harmless cave flyer. Fits the facility as atmosphere.
- **Allay**: blue spirit that collects items matching what you give it and brings them to you or a note block. Could fetch scrap.
- **Sniffer**: ancient mob hatched from an egg, digs up torchflower and pitcher seeds.
- **Armadillo**: savanna animal; drops scutes for wolf armor.
- **Villager**: see Game mechanics (trading). (The wandering trader came in 1.44.0.)
- **Copper golem**: 1.21.9 golem that sorts items into chests. Copper is in since 1.44.0.
- **Baby animals and breeding** for all animals: see Game mechanics.

### Bosses

- **Wither**: summoned from soul sand and three wither skeleton skulls; flies, shoots skulls, breaks blocks; drops the nether star (beacon). The mod already has soul sand and the wither skull, so it is close to buildable. A very large fight for a facility.
- **Ender dragon**: see End.
- **Elder guardian** (mini boss): see Hostile, overworld.
- **Warden** (often treated as a boss): see Hostile, overworld.

## Blocks

### Natural blocks, ores and stone

- **Nether gold ore**: gold nuggets in netherrack.
- **Tuff, calcite**: stone types from caves and geodes.
- **Infested stone**: looks like stone, hides a silverfish.
- **Amethyst geode: amethyst block, budding amethyst, clusters, shards**: crystal pockets underground; shards make spyglasses and tinted glass. Could be found when digging.
- **Dripstone block / pointed dripstone**: cave spikes that fall and hurt; drip water or lava into cauldrons.
- **Clay / clay ball** (partly, 1.47.3: smelted dirt stands in for clay and gives the brick item): found in water;
  makes bricks and terracotta. Real clay would give bricks their proper source and terracotta a start.
- **Terracotta (16 colors) and glazed terracotta**: badlands stone and decorative patterned blocks.
- **Mud, packed mud, mud bricks, muddy mangrove roots**: swamp blocks.
- **Podzol, coarse dirt, rooted dirt, mycelium, dirt path**: dirt variants (a shovel on grass makes a path).
- **Moss block / moss carpet**: lush-cave blocks; bone meal spreads them.
- **Gravel** (has: rare underground veins, flint one time in ten).
- **Snow layer and powder snow** (partly: has snow blocks): thin snow layers and powder snow you sink into and freeze in. Powder snow fits snowy moons as a trap.
- **Packed ice / blue ice**: faster-sliding ice for boat roads.
- **Frosted ice**: made by Frost Walker boots.
- **Obsidian** (has), **crying obsidian**: glowing obsidian, used for the respawn anchor.
- **Magma block** (has), **soul fire on soul sand**: blue fire.
- **Nether wastes** (partly: netherrack, quartz ore and glowstone exist; there is no plain Nether wastes kind of Nether).
- **Polished basalt / smooth basalt**: basalt variants (basalt exists).
- **Blackstone family** (partly, 1.43.0: polished, chiseled, cracked bricks, stairs, slabs, walls, button and plate): missing gilded blackstone as a building block of its own.
- **Crimson/warped plants: fungi, roots, nether sprouts, weeping vines, twisting vines**: the forest floor and hanging vines (the mod's forests have stems, wart blocks and shroomlights only).
- **Sculk family: sculk, sculk vein, sculk sensor, sculk shrieker, sculk catalyst**: deep-dark blocks that react to sound and summon the warden. Natural fit for Lethal Company's sound-based danger.
- **Spawner** (partly: exists in fortress blaze rooms as decoration; missing dungeons with zombie/skeleton/spider spawners and the trial spawner).
- **Bedrock** (has).
- **End stone / end stone bricks**: the End's ground.
- **Purpur block / pillar / stairs / slab**: End city building blocks.
- **Prismarine / prismarine bricks / dark prismarine / sea lantern**: ocean monument blocks; sea lantern is a light.
- **Sponge / wet sponge**: soaks up water. Useful with the mod's flowing water.
- **Cobweb**: slows anything inside it; found in mineshafts. Fits the facility, and spiders.
- **Ice** (has), **water** and **lava** (have).
- **Coral blocks, coral, coral fans (5 colors, live and dead)**: reef blocks.
- **Kelp, seagrass, sea pickle**: ocean plants; dried kelp is food and fuel.
- **Lily pad**: walkable plant on water.
- **Ores already in**: coal, iron, gold, diamond, emerald, lapis, redstone, nether quartz, ancient debris (no list entry needed).

### Plants and farm blocks

- **Saplings for every tree**: plant trees from leaves' drops. The mod's moon trees are chopped but can't be replanted.
- **Flowers**: dandelion, poppy, cornflower, tulips, allium, azure bluet, oxeye daisy, lily of the valley, sunflower, lilac, rose bush, peony, wither rose, torchflower, pitcher plant, pink petals and others. Sources of dyes; decoration on grassy moons.
- **Short grass / tall grass / ferns / dead bush**: ground cover; grass drops seeds (the mod gives seeds from grass already).
- **Mushrooms (red/brown) and huge mushrooms**: dark-place plants; stew ingredients.
- **Cactus**: desert plant that hurts on touch. Fits desert moons.
- **Bamboo / bamboo block**: fast-growing plant; scaffolding, bamboo wood set.
- **Melon / melon stem**: like pumpkins (pumpkins are in); melon slices as food.
- **Beetroot**: crop; soup and red dye.
- **Cocoa beans**: grown on jungle logs; cookies (cookies are store-only now) and brown dye.
- **Sweet berry bush**: thorny bush with berries; slows and hurts.
- **Glow berries / cave vines**: hanging lit vines in lush caves.
- **Hay bale** (partly, 1.43.0): missing softening falls and feeding horses and llamas.
- **Chorus plant / chorus flower**: End plants; chorus fruit teleports you.
- **Azalea / flowering azalea / big dripleaf / small dripleaf / spore blossom / hanging roots**: lush-cave plants; big dripleaf tips you off.
- **Sugar cane, wheat, carrots, potatoes, pumpkins, nether wart** (have).

### Wood types

- **Other woods: spruce, birch, jungle, acacia, mangrove, cherry, bamboo, pale oak**: each with log, stripped log, wood, planks, stairs, slab, fence, fence gate, door, trapdoor, button, pressure plate, sign, hanging sign, boat. The mod has oak (all of these but stripped log, wood, signs and boat) and part of dark oak.
- **Dark oak, full set** (partly: planks; stairs, slab, fence, button and pressure plate since 1.43.0, fence gate
  since 1.44.0; missing log, wood, leaves, door, trapdoor, signs and boat).
- **Crimson and warped wood sets** (partly: stems exist): planks, stripped stems, hyphae, doors, trapdoors, fences and so on. Fire-proof wood.
- **Stripped logs and wood blocks**: an axe on a log strips it; 6-sided bark blocks.

### Building blocks

- **Stairs, slabs and walls for every stone and wood** (partly: every stone family since 1.43.0, cut copper since
  1.44.0; woods: oak and dark oak): the other woods.
- **Iron bars**: see-through bars; connect like glass panes. A good cage or window for the ship.
- **Stained glass and stained glass panes (16 colors)**, **tinted glass** (blocks light).
- **Concrete and concrete powder (16 colors)**: bright solid blocks; powder sets in water.
- **Dyeing wool and sheep**: all 16 wool colors exist (1.42.0; sheep grow six, the store has the rest), but there are no dyes.
- **Carpet (16 colors)**: thin wool layer.
- **Beds, other 15 colors** (partly: red bed).
- **Mud bricks, packed mud**: see Natural.
- **Block of emerald, block of lapis, block of netherite, block of amethyst, block of raw iron/gold** (partly: iron,
  gold, diamond, coal and redstone blocks; quartz blocks since 1.43.0; copper and raw copper blocks since 1.44.0).
- **Glowstone** (has), **sea lantern**, **froglights (3 colors)**, **shroomlight** (has): light blocks.
- **Lantern / soul lantern**: hanging or standing light, made from iron nuggets and a torch.
- **Soul torch**: blue torch, scares piglins.
- **Chain**: hanging iron chain, for lanterns.
- **Candles (17 colors) and candle cake**: small lights, 1-4 per block.
- **End rod**: thin light rod.
- **Scaffolding**: climbable, quick-breaking building aid. Useful for building down to the facility.
- **Ladder** (has), **vines** (have, 1.43.0).
- **Hay bale** (has, 1.43.0), **dried kelp block**, **bone block** (bone block exists in the Nether).
- **Mob heads: skeleton skull, zombie, creeper, piglin, dragon head, player head** (partly, 1.41.0: the skeleton skull
  and zombie, creeper and piglin heads drop from mobs a charged creeper kills and are worn as helmets, but can't be
  placed; the wither skeleton skull is a scrap trophy; missing the dragon and player heads).

### Decorative

- **Flower pot**: holds a flower, sapling, cactus or mushroom.
- **Painting**: random-size art on a wall.
- **Item frame / glow item frame**: show an item on a wall; a comparator reads its rotation.
- **Armor stand**: shows armor; can be posed. Could hold spare armor on the ship.
- **Banners (16 colors with patterns) and the loom**: flags on walls or as shields' designs.
- **Signs and hanging signs**: writable text. Useful for labelling a base or a facility route.
- **Decorated pot and pottery sherds** (partly, 1.47.3: the pot, from four bricks, holds a stack and shatters; missing
  the sherds and the patterns they put on a pot): archaeology pots.
- **Head and mob heads**: see Building blocks.
- **Campfire / soul campfire**: light and cooking without fuel; smoke signals; damages anything standing on it.
- **Bell**: rings to warn villagers. Could alert a crew.
- **Banner patterns, trim templates, pottery sherds**: decoration items found as loot.
- **Pale moss, pale hanging moss, eyeblossom** (1.21.4 pale garden blocks).

### Functional / utility blocks

- **Anvil (and chipped/damaged)**: repair and combine tools, apply enchanted books, rename items; spends XP. Gives XP a second use.
- **Armor trims** at the smithing table (the table and the netherite upgrade came in 1.43.0).
- **Grindstone**: removes enchantments for XP; repairs by combining two items.
- **Stonecutter**: one-to-one stone shapes (stairs, slabs, walls) without waste.
- **Loom**: puts patterns on banners.
- **Cartography table**: copies, zooms and locks maps.
- **Fletching table**: no use in survival besides fletcher villagers.
- **Smoker**: cooks food twice as fast as a furnace.
- **Blast furnace**: smelts ore and metal twice as fast.
- **Ender chest**: per-player storage shared by every ender chest. Could be a per-player locker that survives death.
- **Shulker box (17 colors)**: a chest you can pick up with its contents. Would let a crew carry storage to and from the ship.
- **Cauldron** (partly, 1.44.0: holds water or lava, filled and emptied with buckets and bottles, read by a
  comparator; missing powder snow, filling in the rain and washing dye off).
- **Beacon**: gives an area status effect (Speed, Haste, Resistance, Jump Boost, Strength, Regeneration) on a pyramid of metal blocks; needs a nether star. Strong fit for a ship base, needs the wither.
- **Conduit**: underwater beacon (water breathing, night vision, attacks hostiles).
- **Respawn anchor**: Nether spawn point charged with glowstone; explodes in the overworld.
- **Lodestone**: makes a compass point to it.
- **Jukebox** (has), **note block** (has).
- **Cake**: placeable food eaten one slice at a time.
- **Bed** (partly: red bed placeable; sleeping doesn't set a spawn or skip time, by design).
- **Suspicious sand / suspicious gravel and the brush**: archaeology blocks that give loot when brushed.
- **Vault / trial spawner / heavy core**: trial chamber blocks; the vault gives one reward per player per key. A per-player loot box fits Lethal Company's co-op loot.
- **Nether portal** (has), **end portal frame / end portal / end gateway**: see Dimensions.

## Redstone components

- **Sculk sensor / calibrated sculk sensor**: wireless redstone from sound and vibration; in Minecraft, voice doesn't count, but walking, opening doors and so on do.
- **Redstone lamp, TNT, note block, piston, sticky piston, observer, repeater, comparator, lever, button, pressure plate, torch, dust, block, slime and honey blocks** (have).
- **Item frame, cake, beehive, respawn anchor, end portal frame, brewing stand as comparator inputs** (partly: the
  comparator reads chests, barrels, furnaces and jukeboxes; since 1.44.0 hoppers, droppers, dispensers, the crafter,
  the composter, the cauldron, the lectern and the chiseled bookshelf; since 1.47.3 the decorated pot).
- **Redstone ore lighting up when touched or stepped on** (redstone ore exists, but doesn't glow).
- **Note block mob-head sounds**: a mob head on a note block plays that mob's sound.

## Items

### Tools and weapons

- **Netherite sword and tools** (have).
- **Crossbow**: loads one arrow, fires a stronger, flatter shot; takes fireworks as ammo; Multishot, Piercing, Quick Charge. Bows and arrows are in.
- **Trident**: thrown spear from drowned; Loyalty, Riptide, Channeling (calls lightning in a storm).
- **Mace**: 1.21 weapon whose damage grows with how far you fell before the hit; made from a heavy core and a breeze rod. Pairs with the mod's elytra and slime blocks.
- **Shield**: blocks attacks from the front, disabled by axes. Could block some Lethal Company monsters' hits.
- **Spyglass**: zooms in. Lethal Company has binoculars-like scanning; a spyglass would be cosmetic-plus.
- **Compass / recovery compass / lodestone compass**: points to spawn / your last death / a lodestone. A recovery compass leading to your body would fit Lethal Company directly.
- **Clock**: shows the time of day (Lethal Company already shows a clock outside).
- **Map / empty map / locator map**: draws the area you explore. A map of dug tunnels in the facility could be useful.
- **Name tag**: name a mob so it never despawns.
- **Brush**: archaeology tool for suspicious sand and gravel.
- **Bundle (16 colors)**: holds a stack's worth of mixed items in one slot. Directly useful with Lethal Company's four-slot inventory.
- **Goat horn**: plays a loud call; eight variants.
- **Fishing rod** (has), **carrot on a stick / warped fungus on a stick**: steer a ridden pig / strider.
- **Flint and steel** (has), **fire charge**: lights fires; a dispenser fires it like a ghast fireball.

- **Egg**: chickens lay them; thrown, a small chance of a chick (chickens are in, without eggs).
- **Eye of ender**: thrown to find a stronghold; fills end portal frames. Needs the End.
- **Ender pearl** (has).
- **Splash and lingering potions** (partly: witches throw splash potions at you; players can't make or throw them): see Potions.

## Armor and trims

- **Leather armor** (all four pieces, dyeable): cheapest armor. Leather already drops from cows and horses, so this only needs recipes and a texture.
- **Chainmail armor**: from loot and trades; not craftable in Minecraft (could be smithed from iron nuggets in a mod).
- **Turtle helmet (turtle shell)**: water breathing for 10 s after surfacing.
- **Golden armor** (has), **iron** (has), **diamond** (has), **netherite** (has, 1.43.0).
- **Armor trims**: 16+ trim patterns and 10 materials applied at a smithing table; cosmetic. Would show on the mod's player armor models.
- **Wolf armor**: from armadillo scutes; protects a tamed wolf.
- **Elytra** (has).
- **Knockback resistance, armor toughness on netherite** (partly: netherite armor, 1.43.0, has Minecraft's toughness,
  as diamond does; knockback resistance is missing).
- **Leather armor dyeing** (needs dyes).

## Enchantments

The mod has Efficiency, Unbreaking, Sharpness, Protection and Feather Falling, from the enchanting table only. Missing:

- **Fortune**: more drops from ore. Directly multiplies scrap from ore veins: needs balancing.
- **Silk Touch**: blocks drop themselves (ore blocks, glass, ice).
- **Mending**: XP repairs the item. Gives XP a use; would weaken tool wear.
- **Looting**: more mob drops.
- **Smite / Bane of Arthropods**: extra damage to undead / spiders. The mod has zombies, skeletons, spiders.
- **Fire Aspect**: sets the target alight.
- **Knockback**: pushes the target away.
- **Sweeping Edge**: stronger sweep attacks (the mod has no sweep).
- **Blast Protection / Fire Protection / Projectile Protection**: specialised Protection. Blast Protection fits creepers, TNT and landmines.
- **Respiration / Aqua Affinity**: longer breath / normal mining under water. Fits the mod's water.
- **Depth Strider / Frost Walker / Soul Speed / Swift Sneak**: boot and legging movement enchantments.
- **Thorns**: hurts attackers.
- **Power / Punch / Flame / Infinity**: bow enchantments (bows are in).
- **Multishot / Piercing / Quick Charge**: crossbow enchantments.
- **Loyalty / Riptide / Channeling / Impaling**: trident enchantments.
- **Density / Breach / Wind Burst**: mace enchantments.
- **Luck of the Sea / Lure**: fishing rod enchantments (fishing exists).
- **Curse of Binding / Curse of Vanishing**: curses from loot.
- **Enchanted books, anvil combining, grindstone removal, treasure-only enchantments from loot and trading**: the ways to get the above.
- **Enchanting fishing rods, hoes, shears, flint and steel, elytra** (the mod's targets are tools, swords, armor and boots).

## Potions and effects

The mod has Swiftness, Strength, Fire Resistance and Night Vision (with long and level II versions) from awkward
potions, Poison (spider eye) and Regeneration (ghast tear) since 1.41.0, and Leaping (rabbit's foot) and Slow Falling
(phantom membrane) since 1.42.0. Witches throw Slowness, Poison, Weakness and Harming. Golden apples give Regeneration
and Absorption.
Missing:

- **Potion of Healing** (glistering melon) / **Harming** (fermented spider eye on Healing): instant health / damage. Lethal Company has no healing besides regeneration, so this needs care.
- **Potion of Water Breathing** (pufferfish): breathe under water. Pufferfish is already caught by fishing; this is one recipe away.
- **Potion of Invisibility** (fermented spider eye on Night Vision): mobs lose track of you. Strong vs Lethal Company monsters; needs a decision on which monsters it fools.
- **Potion of Slowness / Weakness** (partly: witches throw them and strays' arrows give Slowness, but players can't
  brew them; Poison is brewed from a spider eye since 1.41.0).
- **Potion of the Turtle Master** (turtle shell): Slowness plus Resistance.
- **Potion of Luck**: creative only.
- **1.21 potions: Oozing, Infested, Weaving, Wind Charged**: slimes, silverfish, cobwebs, wind bursts on death.
- **Fermented spider eye**: the "corrupting" ingredient.
- **Splash potions** (gunpowder) and **lingering potions** (dragon's breath): throwable versions. The mod already handles splash hits from witches.
- **Tipped arrows** from lingering potions (partly, 1.41.0: arrows of Slowness and Poison drop from strays and
  bogged).
- **Effects with no potion: Haste, Mining Fatigue, Resistance, Nausea, Blindness, Darkness, Hunger, Wither, Levitation, Glowing, Saturation, Health Boost, Absorption (golden apple only), Conduit Power, Dolphin's Grace, Hero of the Village, Bad Omen / Raid Omen / Trial Omen, Bad Luck, Luck**. Some come from mobs (elder guardian's Mining Fatigue, warden's Darkness, rotten flesh's Hunger, wither skeleton's Wither, shulker's Levitation). (Hunger is in since 1.41.0, from husks; Jump Boost has the potion of Leaping since 1.42.0.)
- **Milk clears effects**, **beacons give effects**: see Items, Functional blocks.

## Dimensions, biomes and world features

### Dimensions

- **The End**: a void dimension with an island, obsidian pillars, end crystals and the ender dragon; end cities with shulkers and elytra; end gateways. Would be a second special interior like the Nether, reached through a stronghold's end portal (eyes of ender).
- **The Nether** (partly: six kinds, see below; missing Nether wastes as its own kind, lava oceans, ruined portals, Nether fossils, the full biome mix in one place).

### Nether features

- **Lava ocean**: the Nether's huge lava sea at y=31; striders cross it.
- **Nether wastes biome**: plain netherrack caverns with zombified piglins and ghasts.
- **Nether fossils**: bone block skeletons in soul sand valleys (bone blocks exist).
- **Ruined portals**: broken obsidian portals with loot, in both dimensions; crying obsidian.
- **Bastion variants**: housing, stables, bridge and treasure; magma cube jumping in the treasure room (partly: one bastion layout with a treasure room).
- **Glowstone clusters on the ceiling** (glowstone is in the Nether's ground list).

### Overworld structures (would mostly be new interiors or outside features on moons)

- **Villages** (plains, desert, savanna, taiga, snowy): houses, villagers, iron golems, workstations. Could be an outside structure or an interior with traders.
- **Pillager outpost**: watchtower with pillagers and an allay cage.
- **Woodland mansion**: huge building with vindicators and evokers; totems. A possible large interior.
- **Ocean monument**: underwater temple with guardians and gold blocks.
- **Stronghold**: underground maze with the end portal, library and silverfish. A strong fit as an interior (it's a dungeon), with the End behind it.
- **Mineshaft**: wooden-supported tunnels with cave spiders, rails and chest minecarts. Fits as a facility interior.
- **Dungeon (monster room)**: small cobblestone room with a spawner and two chests. Could appear when digging.
- **Ancient city**: deep-dark ruin with sculk, wardens and loot (swift sneak, echo shards). Sound-based danger fits Lethal Company well.
- **Trial chambers**: copper-and-tuff complex with trial spawners, vaults and breezes (1.21).
- **Desert pyramid / jungle temple**: traps (TNT under a pressure plate; tripwire dispensers) and loot.
- **Igloo** (with hidden basement), **witch hut**, **shipwreck**, **buried treasure**, **ocean ruins**, **trail ruins**: small structures with loot.
- **Ruined portal**: see Nether features.
- **Geodes**: amethyst pockets underground.
- **End city / end ship**: see Dimensions.

### Biomes and terrain (the moons stand in for biomes now)

- **Caves: lush caves, dripstone caves, deep dark**: underground biomes. Could replace the plain stone met when digging deep.
- **Desert, badlands, jungle, swamp, mangrove swamp, savanna, taiga, cherry grove, pale garden, mushroom fields, ocean, beaches, snowy plains, ice spikes, mountains/peaks**: surface biomes. Moons would map onto some (desert to Assurance/Offense, snowy to Rend/Dine/Titan, forest to Vow/March).
- **Biome-dependent grass and leaf colors**.
- **Ore distribution by height (y-levels)** (partly: the mod uses depth below the surface: redstone 15+, lava 20+, lapis 20+, diamonds 30+).
- **Aquifers and underground lava lakes** (partly: lava pockets exist).
- **Natural trees of every type** (partly: moon trees drop oak logs; no leaves or saplings left behind).
- **Snow falling and ice forming in cold biomes**.

## Game mechanics

- **Villager trading** (partly, 1.44.0: the wandering trader sells for emeralds and buys a few things): buy and sell
  with emeralds; professions from workstations; trade levels. Could be a second store inside a village interior.
- **Raids**: Bad Omen (from killing a raid captain) brings waves of illagers to a village.
- **Experience uses** (partly: XP comes from ore, monsters and scrap and is spent on enchanting): missing anvil costs, Mending, grindstone XP, bottle o' enchanting, XP orbs as objects you pick up.
- **XP orbs**: visible orbs that fly to you (the mod adds XP directly).
- **Sleeping**: skip the night, set the spawn point, phantoms if you don't (partly: beds can be placed; sleeping is off on purpose since the respawn is the ship).
- **Weather: rain, snow, thunderstorms** (partly: Lethal Company's own weathers; lightning goes for the mod's metal
  items, since 1.41.0 charges creepers and turns pigs into zombified piglins, and since 1.44.0 is drawn by lightning
  rods). Missing rain filling cauldrons, snow layers building up, lightning starting fires.
- **Hunger** (has hunger, saturation, exhaustion, regeneration and starvation). Missing: food poisoning effects (rotten flesh, raw chicken), peaceful-difficulty regeneration.
- **Difficulty levels** (peaceful, easy, normal, hard): mob damage, starvation, zombies breaking doors. Lethal Company's moons set the danger instead.
- **Breeding and baby animals** (partly, 1.42.0: cows, sheep, pigs, chickens, rabbits and tamed horses breed, and the
  babies grow up): missing wolves, cats, llamas, hoglins and the other animals.
- **Leashing animals and taking them on the ship** (partly: leads for any animal since 1.42.0, tied to fences since
  1.43.0; tamed pets and built golems in the ship travel with it, and since 1.47.0 stay in it through orbit): missing an
  untamed animal on a lead coming along.
- **Mob drops when killed by a player only / XP from breeding, smelting, trading, fishing** (partly: XP from ore,
  monsters and scrap, and from breeding since 1.42.0, trading since 1.44.0, smelting and fishing; mobs give XP only to
  a player who kills them, but drop their loot whatever kills them).
- **Taming** (partly: horses, wolves, cats, parrots, and llamas since 1.44.0): missing donkeys, mules, foxes (trust),
  axolotls, allays (bonding).
- **Item despawning, item magnet pickup** (the mod uses Lethal Company's item pickup).
- **Swimming / sprint swimming / crawling** (partly: config option for swimming; no crawling in one-block gaps).
- **Sprinting jump, sneaking edge stop** (partly: Lethal Company crouch; sneak on slime cancels bounce).
- **Shield blocking and axe disabling**, **attack cooldown and sweeping** (partly: tools have a swing cooldown).
- **Critical hits** (has).
- **Fall damage reduced by hay, beds, slime, honey, water** (partly: slime, honey, water).
- **Drowning, suffocation in blocks** (partly: Lethal Company's own drowning).
- **Fire spread and burning entities** (partly: fire spreads to flammable blocks; mobs don't catch fire; undead don't burn in sunlight).
- **Undead burning in daylight**: zombies and skeletons outside at dawn. Matters if those mobs spawn outside.
- **Mob spawning by light level** (partly: no mob within 8 blocks of a placed light).
- **Mob farms and spawner mechanics** (spawners spawn only in the blaze room now).
- **Ender pearl stasis, pearl damage** (pearl damage exists).
- **Crop growth randomness, bone meal** (have), **composting**, **trampling farmland by jumping**.
- **Water and lava source rules** (have), **infinite water source** (has), **bubble columns** (soul sand up, magma down), **waterlogging blocks** (stairs, slabs, fences holding water).
- **Snow and ice melting near light**.
- **Statistics screen**: blocks mined, mobs killed, distance travelled.
- **Recipe book** (deliberately left out).
- **Commands** (partly: gamemode, give, tp, op/deop, keepInventory, respawn, advancements, unstuck, god, and gamerule
  for keepInventory and, since 1.45.2, doDaylightCycle): missing kill, time, weather, effect, enchant, summon, setblock,
  fill, clone, xp, clear, locate, the other gamerules, difficulty, spawnpoint.
- **Game rules** (partly: keepInventory, and doDaylightCycle since 1.45.2): missing doFireTick, mobGriefing,
  naturalRegeneration and the rest.
- **Adventure mode**: can't break or place except with allowed tools.
- **Advancements** (partly: 23 Minecraft ones, Return to Sender among them since 1.45.0, plus 4 Lethal Company ones):
  missing most of the ~120, for example Monsters Hunted, Sniper Duel, Adventuring Time, The End?, Free the End,
  Withering Heights, Beaconator, How Did We Get Here?, A Seedy Place, Two by Two, Bullseye, Sound of Music, Hired Help,
  Total Beelocation and all End and village ones. Many need the missing features first.
- **Death messages in Minecraft's wording** ("was blown up by Creeper").
- **Chunk-based world and render distance**: the mod's world is per moon and resets on leaving, by design.
